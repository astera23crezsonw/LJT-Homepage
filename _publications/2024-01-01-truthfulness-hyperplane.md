We investigate the internal representations of Large Language Models (LLMs) and discover a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside LLMs that captures the truthfulness of model responses. This work provides insights into how LLMs represent truthfulness internally.

This is the first author work of Junteng Liu. Co-authors: Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He.
