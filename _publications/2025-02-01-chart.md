Chart understanding is a challenging task that requires Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to accurately perceive and reason over visual information in charts. In this work, we identify and investigate the perception bottleneck of VLMs for chart understanding, analyzing where and how VLMs fail in this task.

This is the first author work of Junteng Liu. Co-authors: Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He.
