Logical reasoning is a fundamental capability of Large Language Models (LLMs). While recent reinforcement learning (RL) methods have made significant progress in eliciting reasoning abilities by training on verifiable tasks, the scalability of such approaches is constrained by the limited availability of verifiable data. To address this, we introduce SynLogic, a framework that Synthesizes verifiable reasoning data at LOGIC, designed to enhance LLMs' logical reasoning capabilities. The key idea is to synthesize logical reasoning problems with deterministic verifiable answers, enabling large-scale training data generation for RL.

This is the first author work of Junteng Liu. Co-authors: Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He.
