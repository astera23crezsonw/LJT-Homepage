We present C-Eval, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models, covering multi-level and multi-discipline tasks. C-Eval aims to evaluate the capabilities of foundation models across a wide range of subjects and difficulty levels.

Co-authors: Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He.
